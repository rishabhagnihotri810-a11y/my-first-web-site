OUR SERVICES

Complete Waste Management in One Platform

EcoClean connects citizens, administrators and waste collectors to make garbage collection transparent and efficient.

Report Garbage

Citizens can report garbage dumps with location, description and photographs.

Track Complaints

Monitor your complaint from the moment it is reported until the garbage is completely cleared.

Admin Assignment

Administrators can assign reported complaints to available waste collectors.

Collector Management

Collectors receive assigned jobs and update their work progress.

Photo Verification

Collectors must upload an after-work photograph before marking the complaint completed.

Live Monitoring

Administrators can monitor pending, active and completed waste collection jobs.
